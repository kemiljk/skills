# Hiring Humans: match the work and respect the person

Use these attributed examples when the review needs them. They supplement Karl’s bar, rather than establish hiring outcomes or a universal process. [Provenance and reading scope](hiring-humans-sources.json) distinguish guests’ views from Danny Martinez’s framing. Keep ordinary portfolio reviews focused on the work; process guidance applies when requested.

## HH01 — Inspect ability beyond employer badges

[Can AI kill the resume?](https://hiringhumans.decimals.work/p/can-ai-kill-the-resume) · Danny Martinez and Jess Toy · 18 May 2025 · public article.

The authors describe familiar employers as tempting shortcuts that cannot establish a candidate’s suitability. They propose examining work, operating context, and development over time.

**Apply:** use employer history as context, then inspect decisions and contribution relevant to the actual role. A prestigious background neither warrants nor prevents a positive assessment.

**Boundaries:** this overlaps the Jess Toy interview introduced in DM03, rather than independently confirming it. The proposed AI-assisted system is a proposal, not proof of fair or effective screening. Do not infer competence, bias, or authorship from writing style, keywords, or familiar logos.

## HH02 — Relevant work can create opportunity without a public-posting requirement

[How to get headhunted by Lovable](https://hiringhumans.decimals.work/p/how-to-get-headhunted-by-lovable) · Danny reporting Mindaugas Petrutis · 20 June 2025 · reported career account.

Mindaugas built Backchannel with Lovable, shared product feedback, and developed relationships before joining Lovable’s **growth team**. His earlier InVision offer also involved an existing relationship and a timely opening.

**Apply:** a self-initiated project can demonstrate relevant initiative and useful work. Identify the capability it actually shows and distinguish it from capabilities still unproven.

**Boundaries:** this is a selected account, not evidence that the project alone caused the hire or that conventional applications cannot succeed. It is not a designer-hire example. Public posting, an audience, a custom free project for each employer, and constant networking are not portfolio requirements. Permitted existing work can supply the evidence.

## HH03 — Mutual fit needs an accessible, respectful process

[Underrepresented folks in tech…](https://hiringhumans.decimals.work/p/underrepresented-folks-in-tech-are) · Danny interviewing Meri Williams · 17 August 2025 · public interview article.

Meri describes candidates considering whether they are anticipated, respected, and able to participate as themselves. Their examples include explaining interview conditions and access in advance, rather than expecting candidates to discover obstacles on arrival.

**Apply:** for requested hiring-process advice, state what a step evaluates, what to expect, and how equivalent evidence can be supplied. Treat mutual fit as work-relevant information for both parties.

**Boundaries:** do not turn humour, hobbies, personal disclosure, demographic identity, or a preferred social style into competence or “culture fit” tests. A candidate does not bear responsibility for repairing an organisation’s inclusion. The interview’s concerns about AI screening do not validate this skill as fair; evaluate the skill’s actual behaviour separately.

## HH04 — Define the job before judging the portfolio

[Paddy Lambros shares his playbook](https://hiringhumans.decimals.work/p/paddy-lambros-shares-his-playbook) · Danny interviewing Paddy Lambros · 10 April 2025 · public interview article.

Paddy argues that unclear hiring needs can masquerade as a candidate shortage. He asks what the company needs to accomplish, which capabilities that requires, and why someone would want the role. His candidate advice starts with their own goals and desired work.

**Apply:** relate portfolio evidence to actual responsibilities and candidate goals. If an ambiguous title hides materially different duties, state a bounded assumption or ask for the job scope before prescribing missing work.

**Boundaries:** neither prestige nor absence of prestige answers the job question. A target list of fifty companies and personal outreach describe Paddy’s approach, not a universal application checklist. This skill does not authorise contacting hiring managers or sending applications.

## HH05 — New tools invite experiments; forecasts are not assessment criteria

[The future of work: a design leader’s perspective](https://hiringhumans.decimals.work/p/the-future-of-work-a-design-leaders) · Danny interviewing Filippos Protogeridis · 1 June 2025 · public interview article.

Filippos emphasises empathy, experimentation, and improving the quality of a product as tools make prototypes easier to produce.

**Apply:** inspect what a candidate learned, changed, and made useful. Tool output can support that account, but does not establish judgment or value by itself.

**Boundaries:** forecasts about fewer jobs, smaller teams, and solo businesses remain opinions. Do not require every designer to become a founder, use AI, build production software alone, or adopt a particular aesthetic. This article supplies perspective, not measured evidence of a new market-wide hiring threshold.
