## What this changed

The exercise reinforced following project links and explicit credits before reporting missing evidence. It did not justify changing the main bar or requiring longer case studies. A new synthetic fixture now combines three observed decision points: an extractor omits an actual contact anchor, an employer page links another person’s project, and a source-only pass must remain scoped to content. The fixture is invented; it does not claim a defect in these sites.

- Brian’s and Maggie’s homepage HTML contained social/contact anchors omitted by the text reader. That establishes source links, while their visible affordance and operation remain unverified.
- [Chopin](https://githubnext.com/projects/chopin/) credits Maggie and a collaborator and labels the work a research prototype. The separately credited [Repo Mind Light](https://githubnext.com/projects/repo-mind-light/) cannot be attributed to her through team affiliation.
- Short, specific explanations in [Rauno’s implementation account](https://rauno.me/craft/nextjs) and [Jessica’s Audible project](https://jessicahische.is/working/Books/Audible-Pride-and-Prejudice) supplied useful contribution and decision evidence without a standard process essay.

## Limits

No browser surface was available. Text/HTML were fetched; layouts, images, interactions, accessibility, performance, and contact operation were not tested. Ryo’s reader/HTTP access and Dan’s current external agency path could not support a core-work assessment. Those client-specific limits are not reproduced site defects.

This small sample overrepresents established web and design-engineering practitioners. One reviewer inferred responsibilities after reading the sites, which can make role fit too generous. The exercise is exploratory calibration, not a blind accuracy benchmark or a measurement of hiring success. The frozen candidate predates subsequent Design Discipline, Hiring Humans, and video enrichment.
