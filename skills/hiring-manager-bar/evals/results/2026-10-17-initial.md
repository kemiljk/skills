## Failure: a described screenshot became inspected evidence

**Consumer and task:** a candidate asks whether a familiar template should fail the bar and needs to know which parts of the review were actually inspected.

**Prerequisites and reproduction:** use the frozen initial skill with `restrained-site-and-unsupported-ai-inference` from the saved original expectations. Supply only its text, with no image or browser. The saved response is the observed reproduction; a deterministic fresh rerun is not claimed.

**Expected:** judge supplied content and attribute the visual description to the input. Leave actual rendering unverified.

**Actual:** the response says, “**Rendered — supplied desktop screenshot, viewport dimensions unspecified:** The restrained layout has legible type and visible project links.” No screenshot pixels were provided. This may lead the candidate to rely on a visual check that never happened.

**Change:** the skill explicitly limits Rendered to personally inspected pixels or a running page and distinguishes described evidence from an inspection. A held-out text-only screenshot case now makes that boundary explicit.
